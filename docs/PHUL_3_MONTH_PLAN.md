# PHUL 3-Month Routine Plan — Tito Doni Asmoro

**Period:** Week 1: 19–25 Oct 2026 → Week 12: 4–10 Jan 2027 (12 weeks, 3 months)  
**Split:** PHUL, 4 days/week — Mon Upper Power, Tue Lower Power, Wed OFF, Thu Upper Hypertrophy, Fri Lower Hypertrophy, Sat–Sun OFF  
**Coach base:** PHUL Strength Science report, 26 Oct 2026  
**Starting point:** Week 1 complete (4/4, 17,260 kg, 279 min). Week 2 in progress (Mon 26 Oct done, 3,815 kg).

> **Important — how to read this plan:** Weights for Weeks 3–12 are NOT fixed in advance.
> The plan gives the structure + the starting weight (Week 2) + the trigger to go up.
> Actual weights are set week by week from your logs: Easy + top-of-range clean 2 sessions in a row = go up. Hard / last rep not clean = stay. Pain / catching / weakness = stop that lift and tell the coach.
> If you want exact weights for a future week, they will be built from the previous week's data, not guessed now.

## The rules that run all 12 weeks

- **Progression:** Double progression — reps first, then smallest plate. +2.5 kg total on barbell compounds, smallest gym step on DBs/machines. If 1.25 kg plates are not available, add reps or a set instead of jumping 5 kg.
- **Increase trigger:** All sets at the top of the rep range, clean form, ≥1 RIR, TWO sessions in a row for that lift.
- **Stall:** No rep progress for 2–3 sessions → cut that lift 10% and rebuild. Never add sets to break a stall in a re-entry block.
- **Solo bench safety:** Never to failure alone. Leave ≥2 RIR on barbell bench. Use rack safeties every session, set just below chest-touch height, tested before loading. No rack that day → dumbbell bench instead. Smith is ONLY a coach-approved fallback for heavy bench top sets, logged separately as "Bench Press (Smith Machine)".
- **Banned:** No dead hangs / overhead hanging stretches (hurt shoulder). No bench / squat / deadlift to failure solo. No true 1RM test now. No ice bath right after lifting. No chasing 2025 numbers (Bench 60×4, est 68 kg is history, not a target).
- **Volume:** Do NOT add a 4th set to everything. That would push quads to 20, hamstrings to 16, past recoverable volume. Only rear/side delts get added sets in Week 3 (see below).
- **RIR targets:** Power compounds 2–3 RIR (bench ≥2) · Hypertrophy compounds 1–2 RIR · Isolations 0–2 RIR, 0 only on the last set of a safe isolation.
- **Running:** Easy run/walk on off-days only, never hard the day before a Lower day. Weekly distance +≤10%, and no single run >10% longer than your longest run of the last 30 days. Saturday is the long run day toward 10K. A 5K event, if you do one, is easy effort, run/walk allowed, on Sat/Sun/Wed only — not a PR race.
- **Body weight:** Tuesday morning, after bathroom, before eating, same scale. Baseline 90.0 kg (5 Oct 2026). Weekly only, no daily weighing.
- **Health gates (note only, not a target):** No kidney-function test yet, foamy urine every time, high blood sugar. Labs (creatinine/eGFR + urea + urinalysis) + clinician review BEFORE creatine, any supplement, or any fixed protein target. No supplement or protein gram target is set in this plan.

## Mesocycle overview

- **Mesocycle 1:** Weeks 1–5 hard, Week 6 deload
- **Mesocycle 2:** Weeks 7–11 hard, Week 12 deload
- **After Week 12:** Reassess — do not auto-continue to heavier loads without a review of logs, recovery, and shoulder/triceps status.

### Week 5 — 16–22 Nov 2026 — LAST HARD WEEK, MESOCYCLE 1
- Same template as Week 3–4.
- Weights: only lifts with 2 clean top-range sessions go up, by smallest plate.
- Watch for deload signals: reps falling at same load for 2 sessions, soreness still present at next Lower, disrupted sleep, persistent joint ache, dread of training. Any two together = bring deload forward, do not push to Week 6 hard.

### Week 6 — 23–29 Nov 2026 — DELOAD 1 (one lever only)
- Pick ONE, not both:
  - (A) Volume deload: cut sets 40–60% (3 sets → 1–2 per exercise), keep loads the same.
  - (B) Intensity deload (PHUL version): keep sets/reps, cut loads to ~50–60% of normal top weights (e.g. Bench 35 → ~18–21 kg), RIR ≥3.
- Stacking both = ~75% stimulus cut — only for severe fatigue.
- Keep the same PHUL days, same exercises (including rear delts, at deload load/volume). No new lifts, no testing, no PR attempts.
- After deload: resume in Week 7 at pre-deload loads, NOT higher.

### Week 7 — 30 Nov–6 Dec 2026 — RESUME, MESOCYCLE 2 START
- Resume at Week 5 loads (pre-deload), not higher, even if deload felt easy. That is the point of a deload.
- Same template as Week 5 (including rear delts).
- Re-check baselines: shoulder (pain/catching/weakness?), triceps (hot/pain?), legs + running load. If anything changed, tell the coach before increasing.

### Week 8 — 7–13 Dec 2026 — BUILD
- Same template. Progression by the same 2-session trigger.
- No new additions unless recovery is clearly good AND a muscle is below MRV and recovering well. Default: no additions.

### Week 9 — 14–20 Dec 2026 — BUILD
- Same template. This is where the second mesocycle's progression should show: lifts that were stuck in Mesocycle 1 should now move, or stall rule applies (cut 10% and rebuild, do not add sets).

### Week 10 — 21–27 Dec 2026 — BUILD (holiday week — flexible)
- Same template, but holidays / gym hours / family time take priority. If a day is missed, use the missed-day rule, do not stack.
- Do NOT use the holidays as an excuse to do 2 hard weeks in 1 week to "catch up".

### Week 11 — 28 Dec 2026–3 Jan 2027 — LAST HARD WEEK, MESOCYCLE 2
- Same template. Last hard week before Deload 2.
- Watch deload signals as in Week 5. If signals fire early, deload early.

### Week 12 — 4–10 Jan 2027 — DELOAD 2 + REASSESS
- Same deload rules as Week 6: one lever only, same days, same exercises.
- End-of-3-months review (with coach, from logs, not feelings):
  - Total volume per mesocycle, per-lift progression (which lifts went up, which stalled, which were cut 10% and rebuilt)
  - Body-weight trend (Tue weekly points vs 90.0 kg)
  - Shoulder/triceps status, dead-hang ban still in place?, bench safety (rack / Smith fallback?)
  - Running: longest run, weekly distance trend, any injury signal from spikes?
  - Health gates: have labs been done? If yes, creatine / supplement discussion can be revisited with a clinician. If no, they stay postponed.
  - Next 3 months: new mesocycle plan built from this review, not a copy of Weeks 1–12.
